Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Photo Tidy is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and your photos and videos never leave your iPhone or iPad.

1. What Photo Tidy Accesses

  • Your photo library, only with your permission. Photo Tidy reads photos and videos to compare them, measure image quality, and group them by year or place. You can limit access to selected photos at any time in Settings → Apps → Photo Tidy → Photos.
  • Photo metadata, such as capture date, resolution, video length and the location saved with a photo, to sort and group your library.

2. What Stays on Your Device

All analysis happens on your device. Photo Tidy stores the following only on your device:

  • Analysis results, so later scans are faster. You can clear them in the app's Settings.
  • Groups and photos you choose to hide, stored as irreversible hashes.
  • Your settings, the number of free deletions used, and place names already looked up.

3. What Leaves Your Device

  • Place names (Library tab): to show names like "Paris, France", Photo Tidy sends Apple Maps the approximate center of each area of about 25 km in which you took photos. It never sends a photo, a photo's exact location, or any information that identifies you. Apple handles these requests under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases: Photo Tidy Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; Photo Tidy only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid.

4. What Photo Tidy Does Not Do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sharing or selling of data.

5. Deleting Photos

Photo Tidy only deletes items after you confirm, using the system's own deletion prompt. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for 30 days.

6. Children

Photo Tidy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
