Getting Started

How do I start cleaning up?

Open the Tidy tab and tap Start Tidying. When the scan finishes, pick a category (Duplicates, Similar, Bursts, Low Quality, Screenshots, Documents or Large Videos), mark what you don't want, then tap Review Deletion List and confirm.

Why doesn't Photo Tidy see all of my photos?
  • If you gave limited access, Photo Tidy only sees the photos you selected. Change this in Settings → Apps → Photo Tidy → Photos.
  • Photos stored only in iCloud (not on this device) are skipped, so nothing is downloaded without your knowledge.
  • The free version scans your latest 500 photos and videos. Photo Tidy Pro scans the whole library.
Some photos look similar but aren't grouped. Can I change that?

Yes. Tap the gear icon, then under Similar Photos choose Loose to group more look-alikes (or Strict for near-identical shots only). Tap Scan Again to apply. Rescanning is quick because analysis is remembered.

Are the storage sizes exact?

Sizes are estimates based on each photo's resolution and each video's length, so the actual space freed may differ.

Deleting and Recovering

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted, select the items and tap Recover.

Why can't I delete every photo in a group?

To protect your memories, Photo Tidy keeps at least one photo in each duplicate, similar or burst group. If your selection would remove a whole group (for example, when marking photos from Low Quality), the deletion list shows a warning you can confirm.

Does deleting also remove photos from my other devices?

If iCloud Photos is turned on, yes. Deletions sync to your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account.

Photo Tidy Pro

What does Pro unlock?

Pro is a one-time purchase that scans your entire library and removes the 20-deletion limit of the free version. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with. In Photo Tidy, tap the gear icon, then Restore Purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis happens on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
